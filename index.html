About Me

Getting to know me

I've been passionate about technology and computers from an early age. At 10, I enrolled at IT Step Academy in Mariupol (Ukraine), where I studied from 2018 to 2023 — learning the fundamentals of programming, web design, 3D modeling, robotics, and graphic design, and building my first simple websites and games.

After completing the academy, I kept learning on my own between the ages of 14 and 16, through online courses, tutorials, and personal projects. I explored frontend development, game design, digital art and animation, and mobile app development with augmented reality, and I gained hands-on experience with microcontrollers like Arduino and BBC micro:bit.

In 2025 I enrolled at Edmonds College in the United States to pursue a professional education in Information Technology. I'm especially interested in software development, UI/UX design, and building digital products that make life easier for people — along with a growing interest in cybersecurity and 3D environments. My goal is to join a team where I can help build modern, user-friendly, and impactful digital solutions.
